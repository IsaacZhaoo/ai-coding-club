After months of watching complete beginners struggle with programming tutorials, we realized something: AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude have changed the game, but nobody's teaching beginners how to actually use them effectively. So we built **AI Coding Club**.

<!--truncate-->

## The Problem We're Solving

Learning to code has never been more accessible, thanks to AI. But there's a catch. Most beginners get stuck because they don't know how to ask AI the right questions, can't tell which resources are worth their time, or end up trapped in "tutorial hell" - following along without understanding how to build their own projects.

We've seen this pattern repeat dozens of times, so we decided to fix it.

## What You'll Find Here

**Curated Resources** - We hand-pick quality learning materials for beginners. No overwhelming 100+ link dumps. Just the stuff that actually works, organized by language and skill level.

**Clear Learning Path** - Our roadmap takes you from absolute zero to employable developer, with realistic timelines and AI-assisted techniques that actually speed up your learning without creating gaps in your knowledge.

**Practical Prompt Engineering** - Learn how to get better code from ChatGPT and Claude. We'll show you the exact prompts that work, not just theory about how AI works.

**Quick Start Demo** - See AI in action with our CSV → Markdown conversion tutorial. Takes 30 seconds, no signup required, shows you what's possible.

## Our Philosophy

We believe in:

- **Quality over quantity** - 10 great resources beat 100 mediocre ones
- **Learning by building** - Real projects, not endless tutorials
- **AI as a tool** - Not magic, not a replacement for understanding
- **Realistic timelines** - No "learn coding in 30 days" promises
- **Open knowledge** - Everything we share is free and accessible

## Who This Is For

This platform is built for complete beginners with zero coding experience who want to use AI as their learning companion. If you've tried coding before and got stuck, or if you're just curious about AI-assisted development, you're in the right place.
