# Welcome to AI Coding Club

**Your curated path from zero to AI-powered developer**

## What is AI Coding Club?

AI Coding Club is a carefully curated platform designed to help you learn coding with AI assistance. We cut through the noise and provide only the best resources, guides, and tools to accelerate your learning journey.

## Why AI Coding?

Traditional coding education can be slow and overwhelming. With AI coding assistants, you can:
- Build real projects from day one
- Learn by doing, not just watching
- Get instant help when you're stuck
- Understand concepts through practice

## Our Curation Model

We believe in quality over quantity. Every resource on this platform is:
✓ **Tested** - We've personally reviewed each resource
✓ **Rated** - Clear ratings based on effectiveness
✓ **Explained** - Why we recommend it and what you'll learn

## The Three Stages

### [Stage 0: Vibecoding](/docs/roadmap/stage0/)

### [Stage 1: Reality Check](/docs/roadmap/stage1/)
Understand AI's limitations and develop best practices. Build sustainable coding habits.

### [Stage 2: Context & Architecture](/docs/roadmap/stage2/)
