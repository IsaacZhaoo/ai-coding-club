# Community & Support

Welcome to the AI Coding Club community! This is a space for learners, developers, and AI enthusiasts to connect, share, and grow together.

## 💬 Get Help & Ask Questions

### GitHub Issues
Report bugs, request features, or ask technical questions.

🔗 [Open an Issue](https://github.com/IsaacZhaoo/aiCodingClub/issues)

## 🤝 How to Participate

### Share Your Experience
- Write about your AI coding journey
- Share tips and tricks you've discovered
- Document challenges you've overcome

### Help Others
- Answer questions from beginners
- Review and suggest improvements to content
- Share useful resources you've found

### Contribute Content
- Submit tutorials or guides
- Translate content to other languages
- Create examples and demos

## 📖 Community Guidelines

### Be Respectful
- Treat everyone with kindness and respect
- Welcome newcomers and beginners
- Provide constructive feedback

### Stay On Topic
- Keep discussions relevant to AI-assisted coding
- Share practical, actionable advice
- Avoid promotional content

### Give Credit
- Attribute sources when sharing others' work
- Acknowledge helpful community members
- Share what worked for you

## 🌟 Success Stories

Share your success story with the community! We'd love to hear about:
- Projects you've built with AI assistance
- Problems you've solved
- Skills you've developed
- Career progress you've made
