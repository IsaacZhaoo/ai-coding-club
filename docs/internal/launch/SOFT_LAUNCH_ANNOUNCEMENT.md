### Full Announcement Content

# 🎉 Introducing: AI-Enhanced Learning Roadmap v1.1

**Learn to code with AI as your guide - no bootcamp, no pressure, just curiosity.**

We've enhanced our learning roadmap with a new philosophy: **"点到为止"** (just enough depth).

This isn't about becoming a professional developer in 12 weeks. It's about sparking your interest, teaching you "够用" (good enough) skills, and helping you build real things with AI.

---

## 🆕 What's New in v1.1

### 🤖 AI Collaboration Techniques
Learn **HOW** to work with AI, not just use it. Every level includes:
- Clear guidance on "How AI Helps You"
- Specific prompts you can copy and paste
- Tips on when to use AI vs. when to learn fundamentals

### 💬 25+ Actionable Prompt Templates
No more staring at a blank ChatGPT window wondering what to ask. We provide:
- Learning prompts: "Teach me X in simple terms"
- Debugging prompts: "Why doesn't this work?"
- Code generation prompts: "Write a function to..."

**Bilingual support:** Every prompt has both English (recommended) and Chinese versions.

### 🎯 "Next Steps" Guidance System
Not sure what to do after Stage 0? We guide you through **4 learning paths**:
- **Path A:** Continue with our roadmap (Stage 1: Reality Check)
- **Path B:** Explore computer science fundamentals (Harvard CS50, MIT OCW)
- **Path C:** Focus on your domain (Data Science, Design, Marketing, Research)
- **Path D:** Deep dive into AI coding tools (Cursor, Copilot, AI workflows)

### 📈 Progress Tracking (Lightweight)
Simple recommendations for tracking your journey:
- GitHub Projects for visual progress
- No pressure, no deadlines - just clarity on where you are

## 🎯 Who This Is For

**Perfect for you if:**
- ✅ You're **curious** about coding but unsure if it's for you
- ✅ You want to try building something **without committing to a bootcamp**
- ✅ You're comfortable with AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude
- ✅ You want to learn practical skills, not computer science theory (yet)

**Not for you if:**
- ❌ You want job training or interview prep (we focus on interest cultivation)
- ❌ You need structured deadlines and accountability (we're self-directed)
- ❌ You prefer video courses over text-based learning

## 💭 What We're NOT

Let's be clear about what this project is **not**:

- ❌ **Not job training:** We don't promise you'll get hired after completing this
- ❌ **Not a bootcamp:** We don't have cohorts, instructors, or certificates
- ❌ **Not comprehensive:** We teach "够用" (good enough) skills, not everything
- ❌ **Not for everyone:** If you want depth, explore Path B (CS fundamentals)

**We're about sparking interest and providing "够用" knowledge to build real things with AI.**
