## Usage Guidelines

### Legal Compliance
- ✅ **Safe**: Use official YouTube embed codes (iframe)
- ✅ **Safe**: Link to original sources with attribution
- ❌ **Avoid**: Downloading and re-hosting videos
- ❌ **Avoid**: Removing watermarks or credits

### Attribution Template
```tsx
<VideoEmbed
  videoId="VIDEO_ID"
  title="Descriptive title for accessibility"
  caption="Source attribution (e.g., 'Official demo from Anthropic')"
  linkText="Watch on YouTube"
  linkUrl="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID"
/>
```
