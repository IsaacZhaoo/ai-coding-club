## Quick Answer Guide

### "What is AI Coding Club?"
A **beginner-friendly, opinionated learning platform** for coding with AI. Not comprehensive. Directional.

**See:** CONTENT_SUMMARY.md (Top section) or CONTENT_ANALYSIS.md (Section 1)

---

### "What's the teaching philosophy?"
1. Curation over quantity (25 resources, not 100+)
2. Learn by doing, not watching
3. AI as tutor and tool, not replacement
4. Accessibility first (always beginner-friendly)
5. Pragmatic realism (honest about limitations)

**See:** CONTENT_ANALYSIS.md (Section 2) or CONTENT_SUMMARY.md (Philosophy table)

---

### "Who is this for?"
Absolute beginners (zero coding experience), career changers, self-directed learners. Time commitment: 2-5 hours/day, flexible self-paced.

**See:** CONTENT_ANALYSIS.md (Section 3) or CONTENT_SUMMARY.md (Target Audience)

---

- Stage-based progression (3 stages)

- Interactive demo (CSV → Markdown)
- 2 blog posts

**See:** CONTENT_SUMMARY.md (Core Content table) or CONTENT_ANALYSIS.md (Section 1.A)

### "How do I write content for this site?"
Follow the patterns in CONTENT_REFERENCE.md:
- Use resource annotation template for external links
- Follow stage structure template for major pages
- Reference writing checklist (14 items)
- Maintain beginner-first voice
- Include "why recommended" for all resources

**See:** CONTENT_REFERENCE.md (Resource Annotation Template, Writing Checklist)

### "What makes this site unique?"
1. Stage-based progression (not a link dump)
2. Entire stage dedicated to AI limitations (Stage 1)
3. Opinionated curation ("Our take:" on every tool)
4. Prompt engineering as core skill
5. Realistic timeline (36 weeks, not "by next week")
6. Beginner-first design
7. AI as collaborative multiplier

**See:** CONTENT_ANALYSIS.md (Section 5) or CONTENT_SUMMARY.md (Unique Value Propositions)

---

### "What's the user journey?"
1. **Entry:** Visit intro page
2. **Orient:** Choose your stage (0, 1, or 2)
3. **Choose Path:** Roadmap, Prompt Engineering, Demo, or Tools
4. **Learn:** Structured resources with annotations
5. **Progress:** Clear "What's Next?" pointers
6. **Community:** (Future expansion)

**See:** CONTENT_MAP.md (User Journey Through Site)

---

### "How much should I read before starting to code?"
- Stage 0 Vibecoding: 7 minutes of reading, then START BUILDING
- AI Coding Roadmap: 30 minutes, then START LEARNING week 1
- Prompt Engineering 101: 5 minutes + template, then PRACTICE immediately
- CSV Demo: 2 minutes + 30 seconds = CREATE something

The site is designed for action, not endless studying.

**See:** CONTENT_MAP.md (Information Density Map)

---

### "What's the current maturity level?"
Beta launch phase. Core philosophy and foundational tutorials are solid. Needs domain-specific depth, troubleshooting guides, and community content to reach full potential.

**See:** CONTENT_SUMMARY.md (Current Site Maturity) or CONTENT_ANALYSIS.md (Section 10)

## Terminology Guide

**Vibecoding**: Building with AI by describing what you want in natural language (Stage 0)

**Reality Check**: Understanding AI limitations and best practices (Stage 1)

**Context & Architecture**: Structuring projects for AI collaboration (Stage 2)

**Curation**: Hand-picking 25 high-quality resources with "why recommended" explanations (not 100+ link dumps)

**Opinionated Take**: A recommendation based on actual testing, not neutral comparison ("Our take: Cursor is the gold standard")

**Beginner-First**: Assuming zero prior knowledge, explaining jargon, using analogies

**Prompt Engineering**: The skill of asking AI the right questions to get better results
