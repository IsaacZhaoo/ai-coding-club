# User Testing Protocol - Issue #27

## Goal
Validate the enhanced roadmap-v1.1 content with 3-5 complete coding beginners to ensure the "点到为止" philosophy resonates and instructions are clear.

**Target:** Curious beginners (not aspiring developers) who want to try coding without committing to a bootcamp.

## Recruitment (1 hour)

### Target Profile
- **Complete beginners:** Never coded before or only tried once
- **Diverse backgrounds:** 1-2 designers, 1-2 marketers, 1-2 from other fields
- **Language mix:** 2-3 English speakers, 1-2 Chinese speakers
- **Motivation:** Curious about coding but unsure if it's for them

### Recruitment Channels
1. **Personal network:** Friends, colleagues, family
2. **Social media:** LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook groups
3. **Community forums:** Reddit (r/learnprogramming, r/webdev), Discord servers
4. **Email:** Reach out to early supporters or waitlist

## Testing Session (1 hour per tester)

### Pre-Test Setup

**Environment:**
- Tester's own computer (test real-world setup)
- Internet browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- ChatGPT account (free tier is fine) or Claude account
- Notebook or document for taking notes

**Facilitator Role:**
- Observe, don't help (unless completely stuck)
- Take notes on confusion points
- Record direct quotes for testimonials (with permission)
- Use screen recording if tester consents (optional)

### Session Structure

#### 1. Pre-Test Survey (5 minutes)

Ask verbally or via Google Form:

**Demographics:**
- Name (optional):
- Age range: 18-25 / 26-35 / 36-45 / 46-55 / 56+
- Occupation/Field:
- Primary language: English / Chinese / Other

**Baseline Questions:**
1. Have you ever written code before? (Yes/No/Tried once)
2. If yes, what did you try? (HTML/CSS/Python/JavaScript/Other)
3. Why are you interested in learning to code?
   - [ ] Career change
   - [ ] Build personal projects
   - [ ] Just curious
   - [ ] Automate tasks at work
   - [ ] Other: _____________

4. How comfortable are you with using AI tools like ChatGPT?
   - Very comfortable
   - Somewhat comfortable
   - Never used before

#### 2. Task: Complete Level 0.1 (30-40 minutes)

**Instructions to tester:**

"Your task is to complete Level 0.1 of the learning roadmap. This should
take about 30 minutes. Please work through it at your own pace, and think
aloud as you go - tell me what you're thinking, what confuses you, what
helps.

If you get stuck for more than 5 minutes, I'll give you a hint. But I won't
help otherwise - I need to see where people naturally struggle.

The goal is to build a simple webpage using Replit and ChatGPT. Ready?"

**Link to provide:**
- English: https://aicoding.club/docs/roadmap-v1.1
- Chinese: https://aicoding.club/zh/docs/roadmap-v1.1

**Observation Points:**

Track where testers:
- Get confused or stuck
- Skip instructions
- Re-read sections multiple times
- Ask clarifying questions
- Show "aha!" moments
- Give up or get frustrated
- Successfully complete tasks

Take notes on:
- Time spent per section
- Which AI prompts they try
- Whether they modify prompts or copy verbatim
- How they interpret "How AI Helps You" guidance

#### 3. Post-Test Survey (5-10 minutes)

Ask via Google Form (create using template below):

**Quantitative Questions (1-5 scale):**

1. **Clarity:** "The instructions were clear and easy to follow"
   - 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree)

2. **AI Tips:** "The AI collaboration tips were useful"
   - 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree)

3. **Motivation:** "I feel motivated to continue learning"
   - 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree)

4. **Confidence:** "I feel more confident about using AI to code"
   - 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree)

5. **Completion:** Did you complete Level 0.1? (Yes/No/Partially)

**Qualitative Questions (Open-ended):**

6. What was the most confusing part?

7. What was the most helpful part?

8. If you could change one thing about the instructions, what would it be?

9. Would you recommend this to a friend who's curious about coding? Why or why not?

10. Any other feedback or suggestions?

#### 4. Follow-up Interview (10 minutes)

Ask probing questions based on survey responses:

- "You mentioned [X] was confusing - can you tell me more about that?"
- "What would have made [Y] clearer?"
- "Which AI prompt did you find most useful? Why?"
- "Did you feel like you were learning or just following steps?"
- "Would you continue to Level 0.2? Why or why not?"

**Testimonial Capture:**
If tester gives positive feedback, ask:
"Would you be comfortable if I quoted you on the website? I can use just
your first name or keep it anonymous."

## Data Collection & Analysis

### Data to Track

**Per Tester:**
- Demographics (age, occupation, language)
- Pre-test responses (experience, motivation)
- Observation notes (confusion points, time spent)
- Post-test ratings (1-5 scale on 4 questions)
- Qualitative feedback (quotes, suggestions)
- Completion status (Yes/No/Partial)

**Aggregate Metrics:**
- Average rating for clarity (target: 4.0+)
- Average rating for AI tips (target: 3.8+)
- Average rating for motivation (target: 4.0+)
- Completion rate (target: 60%+)
- Time to complete Level 0.1 (target: under 2 hours)

## Iteration Process

### Round 1: Identify Top Issues (2-3 testers)

1. Conduct testing with 2-3 testers
2. Analyze data and identify **top 3 issues**
3. Prioritize by:
   - Frequency (how many testers hit this issue)
   - Severity (did it block completion?)
   - Ease of fix (can we fix this in 1 hour?)

### Fixes Between Rounds

4. Fix top 3 issues
5. Update roadmap-v1.1.mdx (both EN and ZH)
6. Rebuild and deploy (`npm run build`)
7. Document changes in testing report

### Round 2: Validate Fixes (2 new testers)

8. Conduct testing with 2 new testers
9. Verify fixes resolved issues
10. Check that new issues didn't emerge

## Success Criteria

**Quantitative:**
- ✅ 70%+ rate clarity as 4-5/5
- ✅ 60%+ rate AI tips as 4-5/5
- ✅ 50%+ complete Level 0.1 within 2 hours
- ✅ Zero "I'm giving up" moments

**Qualitative:**
- ✅ At least 1 positive testimonial captured
- ✅ Major confusion points identified and documented
- ✅ At least 2 rounds of iteration completed
